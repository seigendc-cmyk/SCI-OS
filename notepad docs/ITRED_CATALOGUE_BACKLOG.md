# Future Backlog: iTred Sector Catalogue + WhatsApp Community Discovery

## SOT Control

This is a future backlog/specification item only.

SCI POS remains the first production SaaS. Do not build this in the current POS hardening stream until POS is stable/live.

## Concept

Create an iTred catalogue experience for product and vendor discovery.

## Search Fields

Search should support:

- Vendor
- Product
- Price
- Customer price offers
- Location
- Delivery / iDeliver availability

## Catalogue Content

The catalogue should include:

- Vendors
- Products
- WhatsApp Community link
- Cart
- Vendor direct call action
- Vendor WhatsApp messaging action
- Link to vendor online storefront

## Brand Naming

- Catalogue name: iTred
- WhatsApp community name: iTred — find your suppliers and products
- Catalogue should include the iTred logo

## Vendor Price Plans

Vendor pricing should be based on the number of products the vendor wants to list.

## Catalogue Deployment Structure

Catalogue should be deployable by:

- Sector
- Category
- Date

## Product Listing Details

Product listings should show:

- Vendor Name
- Product Name
- Price
- Quantity
- iDeliver availability

## UX Requirements

- Phone-first
- Small but readable font
- Three-dot menu features
- Popup window for:
  - privacy policy
  - business terms
  - warranties
  - indemnity narratives

## Footer Requirements

Footer should include:

- “Powered by seiGEN Commerce” narrative
- seiGEN Commerce logo
- Contact/support link
- About Us
- Onboarding
- Price Plans
- Revenue Partners Network WhatsApp link
- Website link
- Vendors Products Discovery Channel and Community link

## Build Discipline

This should be treated as a future iTred / Market Space feature backlog.
